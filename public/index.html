How to play

  1. Each Core starts at 20 health. Bring 15 cards and up to 3 copies of each card.
  2. Start with 4 cards and 2 Pulse. Your opening hand includes an affordable card if your deck has one. At the start of each turn, draw a card and refill Pulse. The first player skips the opening draw. Capacity grows by 1 each round, up to 6.
  3. Select a card, then deploy it to an empty lane. Units can act on their next turn. Units with Rush can act immediately.
  4. Each ready unit can strike straight across its lane or shift into an adjacent empty lane. Units trade damage on a strike. An open lane exposes the opposing Core.
  5. Strike in two distinct lanes in a turn to gain 1 bonus Pulse. An empty deck costs 1 Core health when you would draw at the start of your turn.
  6. Reduce the rival Core to zero to win.
